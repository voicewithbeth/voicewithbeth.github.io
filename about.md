Hi! My name is Beth. Thank you for your interest in my voice coaching services!


## I learned how to teach:

* Adults, from the United States Department of Defense. As a First Class Petty Officer, not only did I often train new people on specific jobs, but I also took classes in the art and science of training people.
* Unfamiliar and/or high-level kinematic skills, principally from Texana Children's Center for Autism as part of their program for parents of autistic children over the course of over nine years.
* Trans-Voice, from apprenticing under some highly-experienced coaches, picking their brains, combining their best techniques, and a decent amount of self-study.

## How I got into coaching:

As I gained initial mastery over my own voice, I began casually helping other learners get un-stuck in their own journeys.
Eventually, another professional coach convinced me to try going pro.
In the time since, I've focused on developing a more complete and organized approach to the entire picture of voice modification,
respecting all I can dig up (or may have incidentally learned over five decades) about both the science and the sociology.

So, yeah. I'm old, as voice coaches go. I used to be in software development. I promise you it's not too late to get started changing your voice. It can be done at any age. I'm living proof.
