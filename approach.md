Voice training is not rocket surgery, but it is very much brain science.
Metaphorically speaking, we are wiring new features into your brain and nervous system through communication and guided practice
so that you can pilot your voice in completely new ways.

**Education** has a small but critically important role in the process. We must:

* Establish a common language, so that we can communicate effectively during practical training.
* Teach you to distinguish and evaluate the effects of distinct vocal features that we can modify through behavior.
* Build effective, and ideally playful, feedback cycles to keep you constantly improving.
* Give you the knowledge to avoid blind alleys and well-known trouble spots.

**Training** should occupy the majority of most lessons, teaching you *how to do the thing,* one component at a time.
We build each up to mastery, first in isolation and then in combination with other skills.
This ultimately lets you to use whatever voice you like whenever you like, and to fine-tune your sound to exactly fit your taste.
It follows a consistent process:

* Elicit a behavior one way or another. (This may take several attempts, but fear not: I have a deep bag of tricks.)
* Refine down to a matter of pure focused voluntary control.
* Once control is voluntary, then we can work on coordination with other skills.

**Homework** serves to turn conscious practice into automatic habit.

## Typical Curriculum

Each bullet point is meant to take approximately one lesson, assuming everything runs perfectly.
Occasional difficulties are normal, but most people get past them with a little extra time.
If you're not starting from scratch, then I'm happy to move quickly through your existing skills.
I can also handle certain basic remedial work, although I reserve the discretion to refer you out to a specialist if that seems warranted.

## Last Words

Throughout the program, we're trying to build a voice that is healthy, flexible, sustainable, habitual, and affirming.
You should also gain intellectual and physical mastery over your voice so that you can henceforth coach yourself.
