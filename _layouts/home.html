The Voice with Beth Difference

Multi-Paradigm Approach

I've apprenticed under both the perceptual-gestalt and Scinguistics models of trans-voice training. This lets me bring the best and most effective ideas together with a deep bag of techniques. You get training that adapts to your existing skills and works around personal challenges.

About my Approach

Diverse Experience

Throughout my career, I have taught and guided people of all walks of life: College students, neurodivergent people, active duty military, union activists, truckers, first-responders, home-makers, and those too afflicted to work. Every one of those interactions taught me things I continue to use today.

Whether you are 18 or 80, whatever your background or gender identity, I will find a way to connect with you and build a safe, effective coaching relationship.
