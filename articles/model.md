I'm a firm advocate for having two or three "goal-voice" models at least until you're generally happy with your own sound.
*You can change your goals!*
But part of the process of voice modification is to compare your sound against the sound of your goal along several dimensions to dial in your desired sound. With a set of **concrete, tangible goal voice-models,** you can:

* get concrete, actionable, and consistent feedback - both from your own evaluation and from other knowledgeable people.
* chase something achievable, rather than imaginary and ever-receding goal-posts.
* cast aside the nagging doubt in your head about whether you sound `$GENDER` enough.

I also want to make the same comparison whenever someone asks for feedback.
That's extra hard if the person's target sound only exists in their head.

## Choosing model voices:

Generally, we want voice models to be:

* Real people, not cartoons, voice-acted, or post-processed for media.
* Speaking naturally, not singing or otherwise performing.
