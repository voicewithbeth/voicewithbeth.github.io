## Standard Pricing:

* **Value Pack:** Four 1-hour sessions for $150 (USD) *paid in advance*
* **Individual 1-hour Sessions:** $50 (USD) *due and payable within ten business days of the session*

If you are particularly flush and can afford to pay double, then you'd be supporting my studio and sponsoring some of those less fortunate.

## Discount Programs:

**Student Rates:**
If you have a current student e-mail (`.edu` or similar national domain like `.ac.uk`), then everything is half-price.

**Veterans:**
If all of the following are true, then everything is half-price.

* You served in the Armed Forces or Civil Defense Forces of any nation not hostile to the United States,
* Your discharge was honorable, medical, or a result of your gender identity, and
* You can prove it all to my satisfaction. (Get your DD-214 or Veteran ID card handy.)


**Sliding Scale:**
If you are unable to afford my normal rates, feel free to reach out anyway.
I might have some other options for you.

## Commercial Policy:

I do not take bookings with anyone under the age of majority in their country.

I do not otherwise discriminate, but if I get a bad feeling about your intentions then I reserve the right to refuse service.

I currently invoice via PayPal.

Refunds are not offered.
